## 常见问题

### AI 解读是否代表真实预测？

不是。

锦鲤算卦中的 AI 解读主要用于易经文化体验、自我反思与娱乐参考，不声称能够预测未来，也不保证任何现实结果。

### 起卦结果是如何生成的？

用户输入三个三位数后，系统会依据易经起卦规则生成本卦、变爻与变卦，并结合用户提出的问题生成个性化 AI 解读。

### 不登录可以使用吗？

可以。

未登录状态下也可以完成起卦与 AI 解读，但不会保存历史起卦记录。

登录后，系统会保存与你账户关联的起卦记录，方便你之后查看历史内容。

### AI 解读失败怎么办？

请先确认网络连接正常，然后重新尝试。

如果 AI 服务暂时不可用，应用可能会提供基于本地易经内容生成的基础解读。

如果问题持续出现，可以通过上方邮箱联系我们。

## 隐私说明 · Privacy

锦鲤算卦重视用户隐私。

未登录状态下，应用不会保存你的起卦历史记录；如果你选择注册并登录，系统会保存与你账户关联的起卦记录，包括你输入的问题、三个数字、卦象结果及 AI 解读，方便你之后查看历史内容。

锦鲤算卦不会出售用户数据，也不会将用户内容用于广告定向。

## FAQ

### Does the AI interpretation predict the future?

No.

Koi Fortune is designed for cultural exploration, self-reflection, and entertainment. AI-generated interpretations do not claim to predict or guarantee real-world outcomes.

### How are readings generated?

Users enter three three-digit numbers. The app applies its I Ching-inspired casting rules to generate an original hexagram, changing line, and relating hexagram, then uses the result together with the user's question to generate a personalized AI interpretation.

### Can I use the app without signing in?

Yes.

You can complete a reading without signing in, but your reading history will not be saved.

If you sign in, reading records associated with your account are stored so that you can view them later.

### What should I do if AI interpretation fails?

Please check your internet connection and try again.

If the AI service is temporarily unavailable, the app may provide a local interpretation based on its built-in I Ching content.

If the issue continues, contact us using the email above.

## Privacy

Koi Fortune respects your privacy.

If you use the app without signing in, your reading history is not saved. If you create an account and sign in, the app stores reading records associated with your account, including your question, the three numbers you entered, hexagram results, and AI-generated interpretation, so that you can view your history later.

To generate personalized AI interpretations, your question and relevant hexagram information are sent to the **DeepSeek API** for processing. Only information necessary to provide the interpretation is transmitted. We do not intentionally send your account password or other unnecessary private information to the AI service.

The app also uses a device identifier to enforce daily usage limits.

If you create an account, the app stores your username and a hashed version of your password rather than the password in plain text.

Please avoid entering passwords, government identification numbers, banking information, precise addresses, or other sensitive personal information in your questions.

Koi Fortune does not sell user data or use user content for targeted advertising.
